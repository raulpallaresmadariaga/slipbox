## The Ruthless Elimination of Hurry

The author cites Corrie ten Boom, who said that if the devil can't make you sin, he'll make you busy, since it cuts off your connection to God. I believe this to be true since whenever I go through periods when I forget myself and start working only, and only focusing on my career, I get apart from God, and that's when I start to feel most overwhelmed, anxious, stressed, angry, and out of control. I find that these are terrible periods of time in my life.
