The recommended entry point into each day is prayer and scripture, in that order, before anything else — specifically before the phone. The reasoning: prayer sets emotional equilibrium, and scripture sets how the rest of the day's inputs get interpreted. Reversing the order — phone first — means the day's frame gets set by whatever the phone happens to serve up instead.
