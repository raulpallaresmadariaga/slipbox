Jesus's pattern of withdrawing to solitary places to pray — often very early, before the day started — wasn't incidental to his ministry. The author notes that he repeatedly came out of the wilderness with renewed clarity about his identity and calling, having been in a place of emotional equilibrium and spiritual grounding. Solitude functioned as the mechanism, not just the setting, for that clarity.
