There's a specific pattern I recognize in myself: when I narrow down to only my career — no space for God, no space for anything outside output — I don't just get busy, I get apart from God. And that's exactly when the worst symptoms show up: overwhelm, anxiety, stress, anger, feeling out of control. The distance isn't a side effect of the intensity; it's the mechanism producing the intensity.
