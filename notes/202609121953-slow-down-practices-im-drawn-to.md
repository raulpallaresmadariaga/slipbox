From Comer's list of ways to slow down, the ones I'm actually drawn to: driving at the speed limit, not texting and driving, showing up 10 minutes early, turning my smartphone into a dumb phone, setting fixed times for email, capping social media, single-tasking, taking real vacations, cooking my own food, walking slower, and praying. It's a mix of small mechanical changes and one spiritual one — the list itself matters less than actually picking a few and running them long enough to notice a difference.
