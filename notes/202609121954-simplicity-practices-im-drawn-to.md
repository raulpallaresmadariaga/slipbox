Comer frames simplicity as a form of minimalism, and out of his suggestions the ones I actually feel drawn to are: opting for fewer, better-quality things instead of more; giving things away; living by an actual budget; and appreciating creation and simple pleasures rather than treating them as beneath my attention. Not the full list — just the parts that feel like they'd actually change how I live, not just how I talk about how I live.
