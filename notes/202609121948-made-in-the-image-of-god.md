Being made in the image of God implies real potential — not only the potential to achieve, but to flourish and thrive. But that potential comes with a built-in boundary: limitation. Not being God, being mortal, isn't a design flaw to route around; it's precisely where God's will for a life tends to be found — in the limits, not despite them.
