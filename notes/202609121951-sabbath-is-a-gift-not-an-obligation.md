Even God rested on the seventh day — and the Sabbath was made for people, as a gift, not a rule imposed on them. It functions as a holy day whose purpose is to redirect the heart toward gratitude and toward the reality of God's goodness, and functions practically as a detox from an otherwise relentless, mundane pace. There's even evidence tying Sabbath practice to longevity.
