I quit Facebook in college and never looked back — so it's strange that I can't do the same with Instagram. The honest answer is that it gives me a fake sense of motivation: the gym, the car, the clothes, someone else's life staged as inspiration. But what actually happens is I trade my own gratitude, joy, and contentment for greed over someone else's curated life. Motivation that depends on comparison isn't motivation — it's a slow leak of what I already have.
