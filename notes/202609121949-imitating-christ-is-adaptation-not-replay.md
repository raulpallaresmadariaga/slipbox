Living the Christian way isn't an attempt to live Jesus's actual life — different time, different circumstances, different calling. It's living as if Jesus were living your life: taking his character and asking what it would look like adapted into your specific circumstances, rather than importing his circumstances wholesale. The operative question isn't "what did Jesus do" but "what would he do in my shoes."
