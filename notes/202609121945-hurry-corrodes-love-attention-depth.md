Hurry isn't the same thing as being busy. Busyness is a schedule condition; hurry is a spiritual one — a state that corrodes love, attention, and depth regardless of how much is actually on the calendar. Modern life is built, deliberately, for speed. And a hurried person cannot fully love God or other people while hurried — not because love and speed are incompatible in principle, but because hurry is itself a character deformation: it reshapes what a person is capable of paying attention to.
