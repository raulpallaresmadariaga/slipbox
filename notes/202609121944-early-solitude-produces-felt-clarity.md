The mornings I've actually woken up early to pray — and there have been very few — the difference is unmistakable. Not theoretical, not something I have to talk myself into believing: I feel it. Which makes the math strange: if a single isolated instance produces that much clarity, what would an actual habit produce? I don't give these moments anywhere near the weight they deserve.
